Why SharePlate?

Every day, edible food can go to waste while people around us struggle to access enough food.

🍽️

Surplus Food

Restaurants, hotels, events and households can share safe surplus food instead of throwing it away.

🔎

Find Nearby Food

🤝

NGO Support

NGOs can connect with donors and distribute surplus food to people they already support.

How It Works

A simple connection between surplus food and people who need it.

🏪

1. Donor Posts Food

A donor enters the food details, quantity, location and available-until time.

📍

2. People Find It

Individuals or NGOs can see available surplus food near them.

🍱

3. Food Is Collected

Individuals collect the food directly, or an NGO collects it for community distribution.

Our Mission

To create a simple digital bridge between surplus food, communities and people in need.
